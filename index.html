關於我們About Us

以運動連結人，
以教育影響生命。Connecting people through sport,
shaping lives through education.

成立樂果運動是為了提供台灣兒童、青少年一個安全、容錯的運動環境，鼓勵孩子英語口說、自信表達。

藉由團隊運動成為媒介，邀請國際師資與豐富經驗的台灣隊輔老師合作，我們不單單只是教孩子打球而已，我們更注重孩子在品格態度上的成長。

用生命影響生命，建立新一世代的榜樣與領袖。

Let's Go Sports (樂果運動) exists to give Taiwanese children and teens a safe sports environment where mistakes are okay, encouraging them to speak English and express themselves with confidence.

Using team sports as the medium, international teachers work alongside experienced Taiwanese team leaders. We teach more than ball skills — we care about growth in character and attitude.

Life touching life, building a new generation of role models and leaders.

營隊日常Camp Life

一日行程A Day at Camp

每日行程都經過詳細安排：從早上的英文閱讀開啟一整天，每天都有不同的動態、靜態課程。

  • 英文閱讀與英語課程
  • 運動訓練：籃球、足球、跑步與團隊遊戲
  • 手工藝、英語歌唱
  • 每項活動最後由老師帶領反思與分享

Every day is carefully planned: it starts with English reading, and each day mixes active and quiet sessions.

  • English reading and lessons
  • Sports training: basketball, football, running and team games
  • Crafts and English songs
  • Each activity ends with guided reflection

主辦單位Our Organization

撒種行動協會The Sowers Movement

Let's Go 營隊由撒種行動協會（The Sowers Movement）舉辦。這是一個在台灣登記立案的非營利組織，透過英文、故事、詩歌與運動陪伴台灣的孩子。

營隊由海外母語人士團隊與台灣在地志工共同組成：海外老師負責教學與帶領，台灣夥伴負責翻譯、行政與家長聯繫。多數志工為無償服事，讓營隊費用得以維持親民。

撒種行動協會不隸屬單一教會，並與台灣在地教會合作。課程包含聖經故事與敬拜，孩子可自由選擇是否參與，我們不會給孩子任何壓力。

Let's Go camps are run by The Sowers Movement (撒種行動協會), a registered Taiwanese non-profit that walks alongside children through English, story, song and sport.

Camps are staffed by overseas native English-speaking teams and local Taiwanese volunteers: visitors teach and coach; Taiwanese partners interpret, organize and stay in touch with families. Most volunteers are unpaid, which keeps camp costs low.

The Sowers Movement is independent of any single denomination and works with local Taiwanese churches. Lessons include Bible stories and worship; children are free to join in, and no child is pressured.
